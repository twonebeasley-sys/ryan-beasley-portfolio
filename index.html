About the Artist

Ryan Beasley is a middle school student at Mount Royal Middle School in Baltimore, Maryland. Her artwork is inspired by manga and anime, and she works both digitally and by hand with colored pencil and marker. Through character portraits, she explores expression, mood and personal style.

Ryan hopes to continue developing her skills in the Visual Arts program at the Baltimore School for the Arts.
