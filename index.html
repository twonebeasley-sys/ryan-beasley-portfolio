About the Artist

Ryan Beasley is a middle school student at Mount Royal Middle School in Baltimore, Maryland. Their artwork is inspired by manga and anime, and they work both digitally and by hand with colored pencil and marker. Through character portraits, they explore expression, mood and personal style.

Ryan hopes to continue developing their skills in the Visual Arts program at the Baltimore School for the Arts.
